| tempo t | ratio r=1/t | out_len | 期望 | 时长门禁G1(±2%) | CPU  | centroid | 质心门禁G2(±5%) | 谱L1 | 自洽G3 | 结论 |
| 1.00 | 1.00 | 44100 | 44100 | PASS | 0.1ms | 444.2Hz | PASS | 0.0000 | PASS(≤0.05) | PASS |
| 0.80 | 1.25 | 55125 | 55125 | PASS | 37.1ms | 443.9Hz | PASS | 0.0051 | n/a | PASS |
| 1.25 | 0.80 | 35280 | 35280 | PASS | 43.7ms | 443.1Hz | PASS | 0.0020 | n/a | PASS |

解读：三档输出长度逐采样精确（exact by construction，与上游宣称一致）；
